About Gianni's Plumbing

Professional local plumbing service built on experience, honesty, and dependable work.

Gianni's Plumbing is a small business proudly run out of Rhode Island, serving homeowners with responsive service, clear communication, and workmanship backed by 10 years of hands-on plumbing experience.

Local Small Business

Trusted residential plumbing service with Rhode Island roots.

As a locally operated plumbing business, we focus on reliable service, respectful communication, and clean, professional results for every customer. We understand that homeowners want straightforward answers, fair pricing, and work completed correctly the first time.

With 10 years of plumbing experience, Gianni's Plumbing handles repairs, drain issues, fixture replacements, and installation work with attention to detail and long-term performance in mind. From the first call to the final walkthrough, our goal is to make the process smooth, professional, and dependable.

We take pride in showing up on time, keeping work areas organized, and recommending solutions that make sense for your home and budget. That small-business approach is what helps us build lasting trust with customers throughout Rhode Island.
