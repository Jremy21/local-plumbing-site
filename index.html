Your Questions, Answered

FAQ

What plumbing issues should I call about right away?

You should contact a plumber immediately for active leaks, burst pipes, backed-up drains, overflowing toilets, no water service, or any plumbing problem that could quickly cause water damage to your home.

How do you handle a typical leak repair visit?

We inspect the affected area, find the source of the leak, explain the repair clearly, and complete the fix using the right replacement parts. After the repair, we test the line or fixture to make sure everything is sealed properly.

Can you clear slow or clogged drains?

Yes. We handle slow drains, common clogs, and drainage backups by identifying the blockage and using the right method to clear the line safely and restore normal flow.

Do you install new plumbing fixtures and replacements?

Yes. We install and replace plumbing fixtures such as faucets, sinks, toilets, shutoff valves, and other residential plumbing components, making sure everything is connected and working correctly before the job is finished.

What areas do you serve?

Gianni's Plumbing serves homeowners throughout Rhode Island and nearby Southeast Massachusetts, with service focused on reliable scheduling, professional workmanship, and clear communication.
